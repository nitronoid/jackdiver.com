Typography: “quotes”, ‘quotes’ and don’t

“Double quotes” and ‘single quotes’ open after a blank and close after a word, so don’t, (a “bracketed” quote) and a quote after a dash—“like this”. A quote starts a line, so “this one opens” after the soft break.

Dots and dashes: wait… a pause, an em dash—here, then —-, then …., and a lone - or .. as they are.

“Emphasis is transparent”, and ‘strong’ too; after a word”even here” a quote closes, as it does in “this”.

Code is left alone: 'code' "span" -- ..., and so is the target and the alt: a “link” that’s text and .

Code spans, images and notes count as letters: c”closes”, ”closes”, and a note1”closes”.

‘A note’ starts afresh, and so does “this” one.

An escape keeps its characters apart: -- and -- and -“opens”.
